A few things worth knowing

Use a sharp, evenly lit page. Rotate sideways text first and use Straighten for small tilts. Crop away unrelated material. Greyscale often works better than forcing faint text into black and white.

Recognition runs in a WebAssembly worker on this device. PDFs are rendered into page images before recognition; existing PDF text is not simply copied. Layout choices influence reading order. The model’s confidence is an estimate, not a guarantee.

TXT exports include your edits. Searchable PDF exports preserve the prepared page image with the engine’s original recognised text underneath; manual text edits are not added to that PDF layer.
